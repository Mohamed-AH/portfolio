Scholar libraries

Arabic-first · right-to-left

Websites that gather a scholar's lessons, lectures and books — often scattered across YouTube, Telegram and older sites — into one place to browse, search and listen. They share the same aims: almost no running cost, safe editing for people who don't code, and pages that stay quick on old phones and slow connections.

Full-stack · Cloudflare

drwasiullah.com

Lessons of Sheikh Prof. Dr. Wasiullah bin Muhammad Abbas حفظه الله

The Sheikh's lessons were spread across a shared YouTube channel, an older WordPress site and archive.org. The site brings about 3,300 lessons, 24 books and 29 series together, with search that ignores Arabic diacritics. Public pages are pre-rendered by a dependency-free Node build, and audio and PDFs are hosted on R2. Behind it is an admin portal (a Worker with a D1 database) where the team signs in with Google or an emailed code, then uploads audio, edits lessons, and publishes, with roles, an audit log, reports and nightly backups. It runs on Cloudflare's free tier and is tested down to an iPhone 7.

  • Node (no dependencies)
  • Cloudflare Workers + D1
  • R2 storage
  • GitHub Actions
  • Cloudflare Access
  • Python ingest

yaljabri.com

Lessons of Sheikh Yahya bin Ahmad al-Jabri حفظه الله

Built on the drwasiullah engine with a different source: the Sheikh's Telegram channels, now about 2,700 lessons. A Python pipeline merges posts across channels, removes duplicates by file size and length, titles each lesson from its caption or the posts around it, and sets aside anything uncertain for a person to check. Lesson posters arrive through a small Telegram bot. Tested with Playwright and axe-core.

Lessons of Sheikh Maher bin Dhafer al-Qahtani حفظه الله

Bilingual · 3D maps

King Abdulaziz: A History

imamabdulaziz.com · English and Arabic

The story of King Abdulaziz رحمه الله in 14 chapters, in English and Arabic. Every paragraph is linked to entries in a source file with exact quotes, and a check script confirms that each item on a map rests on facts cited in both languages. Each chapter has a 3D map drawn with a trimmed three.js build, with still images for browsers that can't draw 3D. Chapters are plain Markdown, so the team edits on GitHub and the site republishes within a minute or two.

Full-stack · Offline-first · Beta

CRM Builder

Modular CRM for small business

Assemble your own CRM from prebuilt modules or design custom ones with 11 field types and cross-module relations, then work in searchable tables or a drag-and-drop kanban. Works offline on IndexedDB and syncs to MongoDB with per-record conflict resolution. Teams share a workspace: owners shape the schema, members manage the records.
